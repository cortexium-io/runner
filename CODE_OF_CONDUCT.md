# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive in every project interaction.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publication of private or sensitive information are not acceptable. Focus
discussion on observable behavior, reproducible evidence, and the needs of the
project and its users.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's Security contact
path when a public report would expose personal or sensitive information.
